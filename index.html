ECHO TRACE 2026

EchoTrace is an audio steganography app that allows users to encode their memories into .wav files of their favorite songs.

Music has the power to poignantly encapsulate memories and emotions in a way that not many other mediums can.

Born from the idea that music can serve as a database for your memories, this website attempts to capture this essential part of the human experience, and allows users to actually embed their memories into their favorite songs to simulate the effect that listening to music has on the brain.
